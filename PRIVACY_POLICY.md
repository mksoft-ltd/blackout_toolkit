# Blackout Survival Kit — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 20 November 2026**

**Publisher: Froggy Eye Ltd (United Kingdom)**

**Contact: info@froggyeye.com**

## The short version

Froggy Eye Ltd collects nothing about you through Blackout Survival Kit.
There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and
no crash-reporting service. We have no server, so nothing you do in the app is
sent to us. The only thing we ever receive is an email you choose to send us,
such as a report about a mesh chat message (see "Reporting a message").

Two things do leave your phone. If you turn on mesh chat, the messages you send
go over Bluetooth to nearby phones running the app, and those phones may pass
them on. And the App Store or Google Play is contacted to show the price of Pro
and to check your purchase. Both are explained below.

## What the app keeps on your phone

Everything you enter is stored in the app's own storage on your device:

- **Prepare:** the checklist items you tick and your household plan (meeting
  points, contacts, who needs help, where the stopcock, fuse box and gas meter
  are, and your notes).
- **Respond:** an active incident, if you start one: what is failing, the steps
  you have ticked, the power-off time for the fridge clock and the water figures
  you enter.
- **Mesh chat:** your display name, your block list, messages you have sent and
  received, messages your phone is holding to pass on, your mesh key and the
  keys of any private groups you have joined. Keys are kept in the iOS Keychain
  or the Android Keystore.
- **Settings:** your theme, whether you have accepted the disclaimer and the
  mesh chat terms, and whether Pro is unlocked.

**Backups.** On Android, app backup is switched off, so none of this goes to
Google's cloud backup or device-to-device transfer. On iPhone and iPad, the
household plan, block list, display name and all mesh chat data are excluded
from iCloud and computer backups. Settings such as the theme, your ticked
checklist items, an active incident and the Pro flag are included in your own
backup, as for most apps. Mesh keys are kept on this device only and are not
synced to iCloud Keychain.

## Mesh chat

Mesh chat is off until you turn it on. When it is on, your phone uses Bluetooth
to find other phones running Blackout Survival Kit and exchanges messages
with them directly, with no internet, server or phone number. Froggy Eye Ltd
never receives these messages.

**What nearby phones receive from you while mesh chat is on:**

- your display name, which you choose and do not have to be your real name;
- your phone's mesh code, a public key created on your phone, which lets others
  check that messages really come from the same phone;
- the messages you send, with the time you sent them and how many phones they
  have passed through;
- for an SOS, the status you choose (for example "Trapped" or "I am safe")
  and any note you add;
- a short announcement with your display name, every few minutes, so nearby
  phones can count who is around.

The Bluetooth advertisement itself carries no name or identifier, only a code
that tells other copies of the app this phone runs mesh chat. The app does not
use your location and does not put it in any message. An SOS contains only what
you choose to write.

**Who can read your messages:**

- The **Nearby** and **SOS** channels are public. Anyone nearby running the app
  can read them, including phones that pass them on.
- **Private group** messages are end-to-end encrypted with a key made from the
  group name and passphrase. Phones that pass them on cannot read them, but can
  see that your phone's code sent a group message, when, and how large it was.

**How long copies are kept:** phones that pass messages on keep a copy for up to
6 hours so they can hand it to phones they meet later. Phones that receive your
message keep it until their owner deletes it or the app clears older messages
to save space. Nobody, including us, can delete a message from someone else's
phone.

**Linking over time:** your messages and announcements can be linked to each
other by your mesh code. Mesh chat is not anonymous. "Delete all mesh chat data"
in Settings deletes your messages and key on this phone and gives you a new
code.

**Blocking:** when you block a sender, their messages are hidden on your phone
and your phone stops passing on their messages, except SOS, which is still
passed on in case it is genuine. Your block list stays on your phone.

**Filter:** offensive words in names and messages are masked on your screen by
the app on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere to do this.

**Bluetooth and location permission.** The app asks for Bluetooth permission
only when you turn on mesh chat. On Android 11 and older, Android requires
location permission before an app may scan for Bluetooth devices; the app asks
for it only for that reason and never reads your location. On Android 12 and
later, the app tells Android it does not use Bluetooth to work out location.

**Notification (Android).** While mesh chat is on, Android shows a notification
so your phone can keep passing on messages with the screen off. It has a "Turn
off" button. The app sends no other notifications.

## Reporting a message

To report a mesh chat message, long-press it and choose Report. The sender is
blocked on your phone at once: their messages disappear from your screen and
your phone stops passing them on. Report also opens a draft email to
info@froggyeye.com in your own email app. Nothing is sent to us unless you
choose to send that email.

The draft contains the message as it was received (without the masking), the
sender's display name and full mesh code, when it was sent, and the channel
(Nearby, SOS or "Private group"; a group's name is not included). It also
contains anything you add, and, like any email, your email address.

We reply to every report within 24 hours. We use a report only to deal with it:
to understand what happened, to reply to you, and to improve the filter and the
conduct rules in app updates.
We do not share reports with anyone, except where the law requires it or where
someone's safety is at risk. We delete reports 12 months after we receive them,
unless we need to keep one longer for safety or legal reasons. Because mesh chat
has no server, we cannot delete the reported message from other people's
phones.

## Contact with the App Store and Google Play

Pro is a one-time in-app purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or
Google, and we never see your name, account or payment details.

- When the app starts, and when you buy or restore Pro, the app asks the App
  Store or Google Play whether Pro is available, what it costs in your currency
  and whether you already own it.
- Google Play's billing library, which the app must use to sell in-app
  purchases on Android, sends its own diagnostic information to Google. This is
  handled under Google's privacy policy.
- Now and then, after you finish a checklist, copy your household plan or buy
  Pro, the app may ask the store to show its own rating prompt. The store
  decides whether it appears. The app learns nothing about whether or how you
  rate it. It never asks during an active incident.

Apple and Google send us only the reports every developer receives, such as
numbers of downloads and sales, which do not identify you.

## Links that leave the app

Tapping a phone number opens your phone's dialler or messaging app. Tapping the
privacy policy opens it in your browser. Tapping Contact opens your email app.
Each of these is handled by that app or website, not by Blackout Survival
Kit.

## What we do not do

- No account or sign-in.
- No analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services.
- No location, camera, microphone, photos or contacts access. The torch uses
  the flash LED only.
- We do not sell any data, and we hold none from the app apart from emails
  you choose to send us.

## Deleting your data

- Settings > "Delete all mesh chat data" deletes every message, group key, your
  block list and your mesh key from this phone, and creates a new key.
- Settings > "Erase everything on this phone" also clears your checklists,
  household plan and any active incident. Pro stays unlocked.
- Uninstalling the app deletes its data. On iPhone and iPad, if you install it
  again, the first launch deletes any mesh keys left in the Keychain.

Copies of messages already on other people's phones cannot be deleted by you or
by us.

## Children

The app is made for adults planning for their household. It is not directed at
children. Mesh chat lets people nearby who do not know each other exchange
messages, so it should be used with care by younger people.

## Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what personal data an organisation
holds about you and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Froggy Eye Ltd holds
no personal data about you from this app, apart from any report or other email
you have sent us, which you can ask us to delete at any time. Contact us with
any question at info@froggyeye.com. You can also complain to the
Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
